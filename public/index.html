About Southside Customs

We specialize in original and NOS Chevrolet parts, with a focus on Impala, Chevelle, and classic Chevy restoration. From door edge guards to rare lighting and interior pieces, we help builders bring their projects back to factory-correct condition.

Based in the spirit of Tijuana custom culture, we also offer professional restoration services for Zenith Dayton wheels, knock-offs, and full custom hydraulics setups.

Currency

All prices on this site are in US dollars (USD). If you pay from another country, your bank or payment provider converts the amount at its own rate.

How to Order

  1. Tap a part and press Inquire via Email.
  2. We confirm the part is available and send the total with shipping.
  3. You pay the invoice by one of the methods below.
  4. We pack and ship your order once payment is received.

Accepted Payment

  • PayPal (invoice)
  • Bank / wire transfer

The exact payment details are sent by email with your invoice. Never send payment before we confirm availability.

Shipping & Fees

Shipping is not included in the listed price. It is quoted by weight and destination and added to your invoice. Any import duties or taxes in your country are paid by the buyer.

Restoration Services

Service prices are starting prices. We confirm a final quote by email after seeing photos of your wheels or car. Work begins after the quote is approved.

Condition Grades

  • NOS: new old stock, never installed
  • Excellent: used, minimal wear
  • Very Good: used, light wear
  • Good: used, normal age wear
