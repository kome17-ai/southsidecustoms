About Southside Customs

We specialize in original and NOS Chevrolet parts, with a focus on Impala, Chevelle, and classic Chevy restoration. From door edge guards to rare lighting and interior pieces, we help builders bring their projects back to factory-correct condition.

Based in the spirit of Tijuana custom culture, we also offer professional restoration services for Zenith Dayton wheels, knock-offs, and full custom hydraulics setups.
