Payment and currency

What to expect before you buy.

USD

Every price on this site is in US dollars. If you pay from another country, your bank or payment provider converts it at its own rate.

How to order

  1. Open a part and press Inquire via email.
  2. We confirm it is available and send your total with shipping.
  3. You pay the invoice using one of the methods below.
  4. We pack and ship once your payment arrives.

Accepted payment

  • PayPal (invoice)
  • Bank or wire transfer

Payment details come by email with your invoice. Do not send money before we confirm availability.

Shipping and fees

Shipping is not in the listed price. We quote it by weight and destination and add it to your invoice. Import duties and taxes in your country are paid by the buyer.

About Southside Customs

We specialize in original and NOS Chevrolet parts, with a focus on Impala, Chevelle and classic Chevy restoration. From door edge guards to rare lighting and interior pieces, we help builders bring their projects back to factory-correct condition.

Based in the spirit of Tijuana custom culture, we also restore Zenith Dayton wheels and knock-offs and build full custom hydraulics setups.

  • OG and NOS Chevrolet parts
  • Zenith Dayton wheel restoration
  • Knock-off restoration
  • Custom hydraulics
  • Impala, Chevelle and Chevy II specialists
